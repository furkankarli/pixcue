# @pixcue/export

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- f5026b9: First release of Pixcue: free motion graphics for video creators that run entirely in the browser.
  - Seven templates: lower third, chapter title, spec card, callout, subscribe button, score card, and pros and cons.
  - Four themes, each with its own look and motion: tech, pop, clean and pixel (with hand-made bitmap fonts that include Turkish letters).
  - Layouts for YouTube (1080p and 4K), Shorts, TikTok, Reels, Instagram posts and square video, kept clear of platform buttons. Phone formats show a finger tap instead of a mouse pointer.
  - Exports for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, CapCut and Instagram Edits: transparent MOV, chroma-key MP4, transparent WebM or a PNG sequence. The MP4 also opens on phones.
  - Optional sound effects in time with each animation, in a style that matches the theme, played in the preview and carried by the exported file.
  - An editor with a live preview, a timeline, keyboard controls, shareable links and saved setups, in English and Turkish, that also works on phones and can be added to the home screen.
